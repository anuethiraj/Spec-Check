      How the checks work

      Every requirement gets 7 base checks plus 1 rule for its module. Words are matched as whole words, and singular and plural count as the same.

      • Vague words: words like quickly, easily, should, may, as needed, etc. or TBD. “May” as a month, as in May 1, is not flagged.
      • Missing numbers: a timing word like before, after, within or soon with no number or month within six words of it.
      • Has acceptance criteria: at least one criterion.
      • Expected results: every criterion says what happens, using “then”.
      • Negative path: at least one criterion covers something going wrong.
      • Clear actor: names who is involved, such as a member, staff or the association.
      • Size: the description is 120 words or fewer.

      Module rules

      • Dues: a criterion covers a failed payment, a refund or a declined card.
      • Events: the requirement states a deadline or a capacity.
      • Relationships: the requirement mentions a term, position or role.

      A requirement can only be marked ready for engineering when every check passes. A flag can be ignored with a reason, and the reason travels with the hand-off.
